## Usage

`gpui:Marker` shows its icon (or the spinner while `IsLoading`) and content as muted `text_sm`. The `separator` class fills the rest of the row with lines on both sides, and `border` adds a bottom border.

## Not covered

- Changing the separator's style (`separator_style`).
