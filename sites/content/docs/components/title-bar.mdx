## Usage

Set `ExtendClientAreaToDecorationsHint="True"` on the window. The theme covers `WindowDrawnDecorations`: the 34px title bar with its bottom rule, the title and the minimize, maximize, restore and close buttons. Avalonia draws these on Windows, X11 and Wayland, never on macOS.

## Not covered

- Arbitrary content in the title bar.
- Decorations drawn by the OS.
