## Usage

`gpui:TagLabel` shows its content in a color: `primary` (the default), `secondary`, `danger`, `success`, `warning`, `info`, or a palette color such as `red`, `emerald` or `violet`. Modifiers: `outline`, `transparent`, `fill`. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Arbitrary color combinations (`TagVariant::Custom`); set `Background`, `Foreground` and `BorderBrush` directly.
