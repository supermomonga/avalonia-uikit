## Usage

The theme covers `Carousel` with `PageTransition="{gpui:SpringSlide}"`-style motion on `Gpui.Spring.Move`: both pages ride one track 16px apart. A `PipsPager Classes="carousel"` bound to the carousel draws the dots.

## Not covered

- Jumping several pages at once by number.
- Reversing a page change midway.
- Looping from the last page to the first.
- Dragging and wheel scrolling between pages.
