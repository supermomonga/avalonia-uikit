## Usage

The theme covers `ProgressBar` in full: a 4 to 10px bar with the progress color over the muted track, the value animating when it changes, and the indeterminate sweep. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Custom colors (`color()`); set `Foreground` directly.
