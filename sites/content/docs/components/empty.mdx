## Usage

`gpui:EmptyState` centers its `Media`, `Title`, `Description`, `Actions` and content in a column with 24px padding and 16px gaps. The `icon-media` class frames the media in a 32px muted square.

## Not covered

- The dashed frame around the empty state (draw a `Border` with a dashed pen around it).
