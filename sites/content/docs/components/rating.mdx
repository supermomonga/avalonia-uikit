## Usage

`gpui:Rating` shows `Maximum` stars and fills `Value` of them in the theme's yellow (or `ActiveBrush`), previewing the hovered value. Sizes: `xsmall` (12px stars), `small` (14px), the default (16px), `large` (24px).
