## Usage

`gpui:Skeleton` is a bar in the skeleton color, 16px tall and stretching by default, whose opacity runs from 1 to 0.5 and back every two seconds. The shape is the app's, as in GPUI Kit: give it a `Width`, `Height` and `CornerRadius`. `secondary` draws it at half strength.

## Examples

A line, a secondary line, a rounded block, a circle and a card.
