## Usage

`gpui:Skeleton` is a 16px bar in the skeleton color whose opacity runs between 1 and 0.5 every two seconds. Give it a `Width`, `Height` and `CornerRadius` for the shape; `secondary` draws it at half strength.

## Not covered

- Nothing: the control covers the component.
