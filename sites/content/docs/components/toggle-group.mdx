## Usage

`ListBox Classes="toggle-group"` draws its items as toggles; `SelectionMode` picks single or multiple selection (`Multiple,Toggle` is GPUI Kit's multiple group). Classes: `outline`, `segmented`, `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- GPUI Kit's selection rules; the app sets `SelectionMode`.
