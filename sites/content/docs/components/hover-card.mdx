## Usage

`gpui:HoverCard` wraps the trigger content; `HoverCard.Card` is what opens, with `Placement` on a side. The card has the popover surface and shadows, and opens and closes on GPUI Kit's timing.

## Not covered

- Opening on tap (`tap_to_open`).
- Turning the surface off (`appearance(false)`).
