## Usage

`gpui:Avatar` draws a circle on the secondary color with a 1px ring. Inside it: the image (`Source`, covering the circle) if there is one; else the initials of `UserName` (the first letters of its first two words) on one of GPUI Kit's twelve hues, picked from the initials as GPUI picks it; else the `Placeholder` icon, the user icon by default. Sizes: `xsmall` (16px), `small` (24px), the default (48px) and `large` (80px); any other `Width` and `Height` stays a circle.

`gpui:AvatarGroup` overlaps its avatars by 30% of their size, the first on top, and shows at most `Limit` of them (3 by default); with `ShowsEllipsis="True"` a "⋯" avatar follows a cut group. The group's size class is given to every avatar in it.
