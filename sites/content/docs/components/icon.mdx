## Usage

The theme covers `PathIcon` and bundles GPUI Kit's Lucide icons as geometry resources: set `Data` to one of them, such as `{StaticResource Gpui.Icon.Search}` (see [Icons](/docs/icons) for the list). An icon is 16px, GPUI's medium size, and takes its `Foreground`. The classes `xsmall`, `small` and `large` give 12, 14 and 24px, and any other `Width` and `Height` scale the outline exactly, as GPUI scales the SVG.
