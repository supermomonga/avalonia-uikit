## Usage

`TextBox AcceptsReturn="True"` gets the textarea's padding and line height, wraps with `TextWrapping="Wrap"`, and scrolls with the overlay scrollbar. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- The search UI.
- Inline tokens.
