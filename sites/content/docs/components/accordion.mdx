## Usage

An `Expander` is one accordion item: a medium-weight title row with a 12px muted chevron (turned up while open, gone while disabled) over the content, on the accordion background. Stack the items in a `StackPanel Classes="accordion"`, which gives every item but the last a 1px separator, inside a `Border Classes="accordion"` for the card around them. Opening and closing reveal the content at its natural height on `UIKit.Spring.Control`, clipped from the bottom; a toggle midway keeps the velocity. Items open independently, and the title row has no hover look and no focus ring. Sizes on the items: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` (medium is the default).
