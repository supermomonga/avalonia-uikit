## Usage

Put `Expander` items in a `StackPanel Classes="accordion"` inside a `Border Classes="accordion"`. Each item reveals its content at its natural height on `Gpui.Spring.Control`, and reverses smoothly when toggled midway.

## Not covered

- Exclusive mode, which keeps only one item open at a time (an app can collapse the others itself).
