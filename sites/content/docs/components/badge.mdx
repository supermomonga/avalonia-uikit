## Usage

`gpui:Badge` wraps its content and draws the count (a pill of at least 14px, at most the configured maximum) or a dot over the content's top right corner, taking no room of its own. Sizes: `small`, the default medium, `large`.

## Not covered

- Nothing: the control covers the component.
