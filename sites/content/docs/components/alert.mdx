## Usage

`gpui:Alert` shows its title and message with the variant's icon. The variant is a class: `info`, `success`, `warning` or `error` (the default is the foreground on the background); the variant color is the text and icon, at 4% the fill and at 30% the border. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Markdown in the message (GPUI Kit's TextView).
