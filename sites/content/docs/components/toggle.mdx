## Usage

The theme covers `ToggleButton` in full. A toggle is ghost by default: no border and a transparent background until it is hovered or checked, when it shows the accent. `Classes="outline"` adds the 1px border and the background. A toggle is at least as wide as it is tall, so a `PathIcon` alone makes a square. `IsChecked` is the state; a toggle draws no focus ring, as GPUI Kit's does not.

| Class | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `outline` | A 1px border and the background (ghost is the default) |
| `xsmall`, `small`, `large` | Height 20, 24 and 36; medium, the default, is 32 |
