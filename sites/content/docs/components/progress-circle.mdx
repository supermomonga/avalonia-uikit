## Usage

`ProgressBar Theme="{StaticResource GpuiProgressCircle}"` draws the value as an arc on a muted ring, animating when it changes, with an indeterminate spin. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`, or any `Width` and `Height`.

## Not covered

- Custom colors.
- Content in the center of the ring (overlay it in a `Grid`).
