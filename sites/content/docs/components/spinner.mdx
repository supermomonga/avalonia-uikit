## Usage

`ProgressBar Theme="{StaticResource GpuiSpinner}"` with `IsIndeterminate="True"` draws the rotating loader icon in the foreground color. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Changing the icon, the speed or the easing.
