## Usage

The theme covers `SplitButton` in full, with the Button's colors, `outline`, `selected` and sizes. GPUI Kit opens the menu under the right edge: give the `MenuFlyout` `Placement="BottomEdgeAlignedRight"`.

## Not covered

- The loading state.
- The Custom variant (set `Background`, `Foreground` and `BorderBrush` directly instead).
- A dropdown caret on a plain Button (use `SplitButton`).
- The toggled state (use `ToggleButton`).
