## Usage

`gpui:ShimmerText` shows its text with the highlight color sweeping across it, in the direction the theme variant uses. The text size and color are the app's.

## Not covered

- Synchronizing the sweep of several texts on one clock (`repeat_synced`).
- An absolute band width.
