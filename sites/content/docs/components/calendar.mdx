## Usage

The theme covers `Calendar` with its `CalendarItem`, `CalendarDayButton` and `CalendarButton`: the header with the previous and next buttons, the muted weekday row, the day grid and the month and year grids. `Classes="small"` gives GPUI Kit's small calendar.

## Not covered

- Showing several months side by side.
- The 20-year grid (Avalonia's year view shows 12 years).
- 4 to 6 week rows per month (Avalonia always shows 6).
