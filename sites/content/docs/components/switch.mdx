## Usage

The theme covers `ToggleSwitch` in full: the track and thumb in three sizes, the thumb's travel on `Gpui.Spring.Control` (reversing midway), and `label-left` to put the content before the track. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Custom colors (`color()`).
