## Usage

`uikit:Stepper` lays out `uikit:StepperItem`s: each is an indicator (a circle with the step's number, or its `Icon`) with the item's content as the label, and a line to the next step. Steps up to `SelectedIndex` are checked, in the primary color, and the lines after passed steps turn primary too. A click on a step's indicator or label selects it (`SelectedIndex` binds two-way), unless the stepper is disabled. `Orientation="Vertical"` runs the steps down, with the label beside the indicator; `CentersSteps="True"` gives every step an equal share of the width with its indicator and label centered.

| Class | Indicator, line, label |
| --- | --- |
| `xsmall` | 8px dots without a number, 1.5px, 12px |
| `small` | 18px, 2px, 12px |
| default | 24px, 2px, 14px |
| `large` | 32px, 3px, 14px |
