## Usage

Give an `Expander` the named theme: `Theme="{StaticResource GpuiCollapsible}"`. It has no chrome of its own: the header is the trigger and the content is whatever the app puts there. With the `reveal` class (GPUI Kit's `motion_id`) the content opens and closes at its natural height on `Gpui.Spring.Control` and reverses smoothly when toggled midway; without the class it appears and disappears at once, as GPUI Kit mounts it. `ExpandDirection="Up"` puts the content before the trigger.
