## Usage

The theme covers the closed `TimePicker`: an input-styled frame sized to its content, the hour, minute and second as two-digit segments with tabular figures. `ClockIdentifier` picks 12 or 24 hours. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Editing the segments directly in the field (focus only marks the hour).
- The opened picker, which is Avalonia's.
