## Usage

`TextBlock Classes="label"` (and `SelectableTextBlock`) puts the text on GPUI Kit's fixed 20px line; a `Run Classes="secondary"` inside it is muted. Avalonia's `Label` control gets the same look and keeps its `Target`.

## Not covered

- Highlighting search matches.
- Masks.
