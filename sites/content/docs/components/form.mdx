## Usage

`gpui:Form` lays out `gpui:FormField` children in `Columns`, with `LabelOrientation` vertical or horizontal. A field has a `Label`, `IsRequired`, a `Description` and the input as its content; sizes follow the form.

## Not covered

- Placing a field at an explicit column (`col_start` / `col_end`).
- Changing the label's text size.
