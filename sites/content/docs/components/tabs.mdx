## Usage

The theme covers `TabStrip` and `TabStripItem`, and the strip of `TabControl` and `TabItem`. The look is a class: `pill`, `segmented` or `underline`; the indicator slides on GPUI Kit's spring and reverses midway. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Closing tabs with a button.
- Reordering tabs by drag and drop.
