## Usage

The theme covers `Slider` with its track, thumb and tick bars, horizontal and vertical, and `reverse` for the reversed direction. The thumb's ring appears on hover and press and fades when released.

## Not covered

- A range slider with two thumbs.
- The active color while pressing the track (GPUI's thumb moves first, so it is not drawn either).
