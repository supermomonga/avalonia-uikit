## Usage

`gpui:Bubble` holds the message on radius 15 with 12 by 8 padding, at most 80% of the room, stretched unless aligned to the start or the end. Variants: filled (primary), `secondary` and `muted`. A `gpui:Message` adds the header and footer around a bubble.

## Not covered

- The automatic rounding of Buttons placed in the reactions row (give them `rounded-full`).
