## Usage

The theme covers `CheckBox` in full. The box is 16px with the label 8px after it; the check mark fades in on `UIKit.Spring.Control` and reverses midway when the box is toggled back. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` (medium is the default).

`IsThreeState="True"` with `IsChecked="{x:Null}"` gives the indeterminate state, drawn as a minus mark. A `CheckBox` without `Content` drops the label and its gap: the theme adds the `bare` class to the indicator on its own. The focus ring wraps the whole row and shows on keyboard focus only, not on a pointer press.
