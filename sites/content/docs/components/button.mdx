## Usage

The theme covers `Button` in full. Colors: `primary`, `secondary`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, `info`, `ghost`, `link` and `text` (none is Default). Modifiers: `outline`, `compact`, `selected` and `icon-only`. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`. Radius: `rounded-none`, `rounded-small`, `rounded-large`, `rounded-full`. The classes combine, as in `Classes="danger outline small"`.

## Not covered

- The loading state.
- The Custom variant (set `Background`, `Foreground` and `BorderBrush` directly instead).
- A dropdown caret on a plain Button (use `SplitButton`).
- The toggled state (use `ToggleButton`).
