## Usage

The theme covers `Menu`, `MenuItem`, `ContextMenu` and `MenuFlyoutPresenter` in full: the popover surface with its ring and shadows, check and radio items, submenus, separators, icons and input gestures.

## Not covered

- Link items (open the link from a `Click` handler).
