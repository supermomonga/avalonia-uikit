## Usage

`PipsPager` with numbered pages: `NumberOfPages`, `SelectedPageIndex`, and Previous and Next as ghost buttons with a chevron and a label. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Collapsing long page lists with ellipses.
