## Usage

Put a `GridSplitter` in a 1px column or row of a `Grid`. The handle shows GPUI Kit's pill on hover, while pressed and while dragging, and the panels resize with the grid's sizes.

## Not covered

- Registering panels and saving and restoring their sizes.
