## Usage

`ComboBox Classes="combobox"` gives GPUI Kit's combobox look to a select (the title is the committed item only). `AutoCompleteBox` gives the filtering variant: the input frame as its text field and the suggestions in GPUI's dropdown. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`.

## Not covered

- Multiple selection with chips.
- A custom search function (AutoCompleteBox's own filtering is used).
