## Usage

The theme covers `ScrollViewer`, `ScrollBar` and `Thumb` in full: scrollbars overlay the content in a 16px strip, appear on scroll, grow under the pointer and fade out after two seconds. Every scrolling control gets them.

## Not covered

- Nothing: the control's look and motion are covered in full.
