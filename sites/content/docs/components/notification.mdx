## Usage

Use Avalonia's `WindowNotificationManager`; its `NotificationCard` is themed with GPUI Kit's surface, the type's icon, the close button and the enter and leave animations. The `Position` and `MaxItems` are the manager's.

## Not covered

- Placing each notification individually.
- Deduplicating notifications.
- Expanding and re-stacking the pile.
