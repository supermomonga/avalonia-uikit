## Usage

The theme covers `Flyout` and `FlyoutPresenter`: the popover background, the ring and shadows, and the fade and slide when opening. Give the flyout a `Placement` such as `BottomEdgeAlignedLeft`.

## Not covered

- The closing animation; a closed popup cannot be drawn in Avalonia.
