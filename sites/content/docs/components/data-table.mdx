## Usage

`TableView` has GPUI Kit's DataTable look by default: the header row, bordered cells, striped rows, the hover and selection colors and the overlay scrollbars. For the `DataGrid` package, add `GpuiDataGridTheme`; the grid gets the same look, including sorting indicators in the headers.

## Not covered

- Sorting, moving and pinning columns in `TableView` (DataGrid has its own).
- Cell range selection.
- Infinite loading.
- The frame around a right-clicked row (Avalonia selects the row on the right button).
- NativeAOT with `DataGrid`, which is not trimmable itself.
