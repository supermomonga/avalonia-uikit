## Usage

The theme covers `ListBox` and `ListBoxItem`: 34px rows with GPUI Kit's hover and active colors, no chrome of its own, and the overlay scrollbar from the `ScrollViewer` theme.

## Not covered

- The built-in search field.
- Loading more items on scroll.
- Reordering by drag and drop.
