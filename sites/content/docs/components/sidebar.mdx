## Usage

`SplitView` carries the sidebar surface and its inline modes: `CompactInline` is GPUI Kit's icon mode, and the pane collapses and expands on its spring. `DrawerPage` adds the locked and off-canvas behaviors.

## Not covered

- `SidebarMenu` and its items (use a `ListBox`).
- Badges on items.
- GPUI's jump to the previous target when reversed midway.
- Unmounting the off-canvas pane 200ms after closing.
