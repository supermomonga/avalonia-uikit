## Usage

The theme covers `ToolTip` in full: the popover background with a 1px border and GPUI's shadow, 14px text, and the fade and slide when it shows. Set `ToolTip.Tip` on any control.

## Not covered

- Resolving a key binding from an Action.
- The grace period before closing.
- The slide between neighbouring tooltips.
