## Usage

The theme covers `CommandBar` with `CommandBarButton`, `CommandBarToggleButton` and `CommandBarSeparator`: compact ghost buttons with their icons, 1px separators, and the overflow menu in GPUI Kit's popover.

## Not covered

- Arbitrary elements and flexible spacers in the bar.
- `ToolbarGroup`.
