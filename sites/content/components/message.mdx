## Usage

`uikit:Message` is one turn of a conversation. Its items are the bubbles (or any content: plain strings show in 16px text), 10px apart; `Header` sits above them and `Footer` below, in muted 12px text inset 12px unless a bubble is `ghost`; `Avatar` sits at the bottom beside them. `Classes="end"` mirrors the whole message to the end of the row. Stack messages in a `StackPanel` with `Spacing="8"` for a message group.
