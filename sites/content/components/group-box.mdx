## Usage

The theme covers `GroupBox`. `Header` is the muted title, and the content follows 16px below it (12px for `fill` and `outline`). Without a class the group sits on the page background; `fill` paints the group box color and `outline` a 1px border, and both pad the content by 16px. Without a `Header`, only the surface remains.
