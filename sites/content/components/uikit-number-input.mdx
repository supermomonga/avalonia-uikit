## Usage

`uikit:NumberInput` is a `NumericUpDown` with the look of the [Number Input](/components/number-input) that keeps its text a number while it is typed: an optional sign, digits and one point, with full-width digits and signs typed as ASCII. `uikit:Inputs.MaskPattern`, `Pattern` and `Validate` set on it replace the rule; a `uikit:NumberMask` with a `Separator` groups the digits (see [Input](/components/input)).

A step (the buttons, ↑ and ↓, the mouse wheel) moves the typed value by `Increment`, keeps the decimals of the value and the step (0.1 + 0.2 gives 0.3), clamps to `Minimum` and `Maximum`, and does nothing when it cannot move the value that way. `StepBy` is a `Func<decimal, StepAction, decimal>` that gives the step for the value and the direction. With `StepsValue="False"`, a step only raises `Step` with its `Action` (`Increment` or `Decrement`), and the app sets the value; `Step` is also raised for a step whose value the input's rules reject.

The demo has steps of 0.2 that keep one decimal, an amount grouped by thousands stepped by 100, and a `StepBy` that steps by 0.1 below 1 and by 0.5 above it.
