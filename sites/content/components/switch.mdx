## Usage

The theme covers `ToggleSwitch`. The track is 28×16 for `xsmall` and `small` (they differ in the label's text size), 36×20 at medium (the default) and 44×24 for `large`; `IsChecked` colors it. The thumb travels on the `UIKit.Spring.Move` spring, keeping its velocity when the switch is toggled back mid-flight, and follows the pointer at once while dragged.

`Content` is the label after the track; `Classes="label-left"` puts it before. Unlike a button, a pointer press focuses a switch, so its ring shows on `:focus`.
