## Usage

The theme covers `Carousel`: it shows one page at a time, clipped to its bounds, and its `PageTransition` is `uikit:SpringSlide`, on which both pages ride one track 16px apart on a spring. Set `Focusable="True"` to make the carousel a tab stop (Avalonia then handles the arrow keys); keyboard focus rings the pages.

The app adds the controls around the pages: Previous and Next are `Button Classes="outline icon-only rounded-full"` with a chevron, 16px outside the pages and centered on them, whose `Click` handlers call the carousel's `Previous()` and `Next()`; the dots are a `PipsPager Classes="carousel"` 16px below, with `SelectedPageIndex` bound two-way to the carousel's `SelectedIndex`.

## Previous and Next

`uikit:CarouselPrevious` and `uikit:CarouselNext` are the buttons above, bound to a carousel with `Carousel="{Binding #Slides}"`. Put them in the same `Panel` as the carousel: they sit 16px outside the pages, centered on them, or above and below a vertical carousel, with a chevron along its axis. They are disabled at the first and the last page unless the carousel has `WrapSelection="True"`, and after a click the carousel takes the focus without a ring, so the arrow keys go on paging. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large`. Content set on a button replaces its chevron.

## Pointer input

With `uikit:Carousels.TracksPointer="True"` the pages lie side by side, 16px apart, on a track that moves on the same spring. A drag along the axis moves the pages with the pointer and lets go on the nearest page; a drag across the axis is left to the page around the carousel, and a drag never clicks a control on a page. A wheel notch along the axis steps one page; a trackpad scroll moves the pages and settles on the nearest one when it stops. A vertical carousel passes a scroll that starts at its first or last page on to the page that contains it. Going to a page several pages away slides past the pages between, and with `WrapSelection="True"` the track goes on from the last page to the first without sliding back. Leave the carousel's `ItemsPanel` to the theme.

Drag the pages, or use the buttons, the dots or the arrow keys.
