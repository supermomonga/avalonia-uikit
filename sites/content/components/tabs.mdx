## Usage

The theme covers `TabStrip` and `TabStripItem` (a bar on its own) and the strip of `TabControl` and `TabItem` (tabs with their content). The look is a class on the strip or control: the default look without one, or `outline`, `pill`, `segmented` or `underline`. Pill, segmented and underline show the selection with an indicator that springs to the selected tab on the `UIKit.Spring.Move` spring and reverses midway when the selection changes again; a pill's label fades in as the indicator slides. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` (medium without one). Put `icon-only` on an item whose content is an icon. A tab shows a ring on keyboard focus only. For tabs that are reordered by dragging, closed and added, see [Tabalonia](/components/tabalonia).

## Menu, prefix and suffix

Tabs that do not fit the bar scroll sideways, and the selected tab scrolls into view. The `menu` class adds a caret button at the end of the bar that lists the tabs, with the selected one checked and disabled ones disabled, and selects the one that is picked. `uikit:Tabs.Prefix` and `uikit:Tabs.Suffix` put content at the start and the end of the bar, outside the scrolling tabs; with a suffix or the menu the tabs end with 12px of space. They work the same on `TabStrip` and `TabControl`.
