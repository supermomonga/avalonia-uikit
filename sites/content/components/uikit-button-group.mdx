## Usage

`uikit:ButtonGroup` is the [Button Group](/components/button-group) as a control. It is a `StackPanel`, horizontal by default, of `Button`s. Its color, `outline`, `compact` and size classes go on every button. A click on a button raises its `Click` with `SelectedIndices`, the buttons the selection becomes: the clicked button alone, or with `Multiple="True"` the selection with the clicked button added or removed. The group does not change the selection itself; the app applies it with each button's `selected` class. `IsEnabled="False"` on the group disables its buttons.

The demo has a group for one choice and one with `Multiple="True"`; each applies the indices its `Click` reports.
