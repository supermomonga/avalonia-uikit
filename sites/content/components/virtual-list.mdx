## Usage

`ListBox` virtualizes with a `VirtualizingStackPanel` by default, and the theme keeps that panel, so the rows and the overlay scrollbar of the [List](/components/list) apply to long lists as they are. Bind `ItemsSource` and give the row a `DataTemplate` in `ItemTemplate`; the demo lists ten thousand records, and only the rows in view are realized. A virtual list looks the same as the List, so there is no class to add.

For sections, a search field or loading more at the end, use [`uikit:ListView`](/components/uikit-list-view); its rows share one height.

## Scroll to an item

`ScrollToItem`, an extension method for `ListBox` and any other `ItemsControl` (namespace `AvaloniaUIKit`), scrolls to an item by its index, also one the panel has not realized yet. `ScrollStrategy.Center` puts the item in the middle of the list, as far as the list allows; the other strategies bring an item out of view to the nearer edge and leave an item in view where it is. A call made before the list is laid out waits for the layout.
