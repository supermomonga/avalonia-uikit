## Usage

`uikit:Clipboard` is a `Button` (so `Click` and `Command` work) drawn as a ghost icon-only button with the copy icon. Set `Text` to what it copies; a click writes it to the clipboard, raises `Copied`, and shows the check icon for two seconds (the `:copied` pseudo-class and `IsCopied`), during which another click does nothing. Sizes are the Button's: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` (medium is the default).
