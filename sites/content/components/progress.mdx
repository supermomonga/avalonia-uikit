## Usage

The theme covers `ProgressBar`: a bar in the progress color over the same color at 20%, with half its height as the radius. A change of `Value` animates the bar's width over 180ms on the cubic Bézier easing (0.2, 0, 0, 1). `IsIndeterminate="True"` repeats a one-second sweep: the bar's leading edge runs across in the second, and its trailing edge follows in the second half, both ends rounded. Sizes: `xsmall` (4px), `small` (6px), `large` (10px); medium (8px) needs no class.
