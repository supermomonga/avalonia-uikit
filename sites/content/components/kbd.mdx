## Usage

`uikit:Kbd` shows its `Gesture` (a `KeyGesture`, such as `Ctrl+Shift+P`) in muted 12px text on a key cap at least 20px wide with 4×2 padding. The text follows the running platform's notation: on macOS the modifier symbols without a separator (⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ⌘, with ⏎ and ⎋ for Enter and Escape), elsewhere the names joined by "+" (Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win). `Classes="outline"` draws the cap on the background with a 1px border.
