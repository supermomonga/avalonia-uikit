## Usage

The theme covers `Separator`: a 1px line in the border color, centered on a box of no thickness, so it straddles the boundary between its neighbours without taking space. A vertical separator stretches to its row's height (the demos give the `StackPanel` one). Classes: `vertical`, `dashed` (a 4px dash with a 2px gap); they combine.
