## Usage

The theme covers `ComboBox` and `ComboBoxItem`. The trigger is the input frame with the selected item (or the muted `PlaceholderText`) and a muted chevron; it shows the ring while focused or open, and stays focused after a mouse selection. Disabled, the frame fades to half; `IsEnabled="False"` on a `ComboBoxItem` mutes that row. An invalid select (`:error` through `DataValidationErrors`) turns the border to the danger color.

The popup opens 6px below the trigger (above it when it flips), as wide as the trigger and at most 320px tall: the popover surface with 4px of padding around rounded rows. The row under the keyboard cursor shows the accent, a hovered row the accent at 70%, and the selected row has a check mark. On every open the popup slides 8px down and fades in over 150ms, and while it is open the trigger shows the item under the cursor. Sizes: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` (medium is the default).

`Classes="clearButton"` replaces the chevron with a clear button while an item is selected; a click on it clears the selection (`ComboBox.Clear()`) without opening the list. `IsEditable="True"` turns the title into a text field in the same frame: typing an item's text selects it, `F4` or `Alt+Down` opens the list, and the frame shows the ring while the field has focus. A right click on the field opens the [Input](/components/input)'s menu (Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All).

For a search field, sections, multiple selection, a footer or an empty view, use [`uikit:Select`](/components/uikit-select).
