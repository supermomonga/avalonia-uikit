## Usage

`uikit:TextLabel` is a `TextBlock` with the look of the [Label](/components/label) built in and the label's features as properties: `Secondary` adds muted text after a space, `Highlights` colors the case-insensitive matches of a query blue (every match, or with `HighlightsMatch="Prefix"` only one at the start of the text), and `IsMasked` shows a • for each character. Sizes, weights, colors and alignment are the `TextBlock`'s `FontSize`, `FontWeight`, `Foreground` and `TextAlignment`.
