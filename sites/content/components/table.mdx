## Usage

`TableView Theme="{StaticResource UIKitTable}"` gives a plain table: a header band, rows sized by their content with a rule between them, 14px text at every size, cells centered in their rows, and no hover or selection colors. The default `TableView` theme is the [Data Table](/components/data-table); both share the cell and header themes. The table has no border of its own: `BorderThickness="1" CornerRadius="5.5"` makes a bordered one. Without rows it shows the header alone.

Columns are `TableViewColumn`s with a `Header` and a `Binding`. `Width` fixes a column in pixels, and `HorizontalContentAlignment="Right"` right-aligns a column's cells.

| Class | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `stripe` | Every other row, starting with the second, on the even color. |
| `xsmall`, `small`, `large` | Cell padding 4×2, 6×3 and 12×8 instead of 8×4; the text stays 14px. |

`uikit:Table` has the same look and is written out row by row instead of bound to items: a `uikit:TableHeader`, `uikit:TableBody` and `uikit:TableFooter` of `uikit:TableRow`s, and a `uikit:TableCaption`, stacked in the order given. A header row holds `uikit:TableHead`s and the other rows `uikit:TableCell`s. The footer is on the foot color under a rule, and the caption is muted, centered text in the cells' padding. Every row lays out its cells on its own: a cell's share of the row is its `ColSpan` (1 by default), cells never get narrower than 100px per column (or their `MinWidth`), and `PreferredWidth` asks for a width that shrinks with the other cells when the row is too narrow, while `Width` keeps one. Rows whose spans add up the same line up. Cell text stays on one line, and `HorizontalContentAlignment` places a cell's content. The table is not virtualized. The same classes apply: `xsmall`, `small`, `large` and `stripe`, and `BorderThickness="1" CornerRadius="5.5"` for the bordered table.
