## Usage

The theme covers `PathIcon` and has Lucide icons as geometry resources: set `Data` to one of them, such as `{StaticResource UIKit.Icon.Search}` (see [Icons](/docs/icons) for which an app has, and the [Icons](/icons) page for all of them). An icon is 16px, the medium size, and takes its `Foreground`. The classes `xsmall`, `small` and `large` give 12, 14 and 24px, and any other `Width` and `Height` scale the outline exactly.

To draw one of the components' icons by name, use [`uikit:Icon`](/components/uikit-icon).
