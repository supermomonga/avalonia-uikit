## Usage

The theme covers `TextBox` (`MaskedTextBox` shares it): the frame with the input background and a 1px `input` border that turns to `ring`, with the ring band, while the field or anything in it has focus; the muted `PlaceholderText`; no hover look. The selection shows only while the field is focused.

`InnerLeftContent` and `InnerRightContent` are the prefix and suffix, a size's gap from the text; for addons inside one bordered frame see Input Group. A password is masked with `PasswordChar="•"`, and `Classes="revealPasswordButton"` adds a mask toggle, an eye button that shows the text while checked. `Classes="clearButton"` adds a clear button while the text is not empty and can be edited; a click clears the text and focuses the field. After the text come the mask toggle, the clear button and the `InnerRightContent`, in that order. A disabled field fills with the disabled background and draws its text, placeholder and inner content at half strength; a read-only field has no look of its own.

A right click opens the menu with Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All and their shortcuts. Cut needs a selection in an editable field, Copy a selection, Paste an editable field; a masked password cannot be cut or copied. The labels are the resources `UIKit.Strings.Cut`, `UIKit.Strings.Copy`, `UIKit.Strings.Paste` and `UIKit.Strings.SelectAll`; replace them to translate the menu, or set your own `ContextFlyout`.

| Class | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `xsmall`, `small`, `large` | Height 20, 24 and 44; medium, the default, is 32 |
| `revealPasswordButton` | The eye button that reveals a masked password |
| `clearButton` | The x button that clears the text |
