## Usage

The theme covers `NumericUpDown` (and `ButtonSpinner`): the input frame with the decrease button before the value, the text centred between the buttons, and the increase button after it. The buttons stay at the sides by contract: `ButtonSpinnerLocation` has no effect. `Value`, `Minimum`, `Maximum`, `Increment` and `FormatString` are Avalonia's own. `InnerLeftContent` and `InnerRightContent` are a prefix and a suffix, right beside the value. The frame shows the ring while anything in it has focus; a disabled field fades as a whole to 50%. Sizes: `xsmall` (20px), `small` (24px), medium (32px, the default) and `large` (44px).

## uikit:NumberInput

`uikit:NumberInput` is a `NumericUpDown` with the same look that keeps its text a number while it is typed: an optional sign, digits and one point, with full-width digits and signs typed as ASCII. `uikit:Inputs.MaskPattern`, `Pattern` and `Validate` set on it replace the rule; a `uikit:NumberMask` with a `Separator` groups the digits.

A step (the buttons, ↑ and ↓, the mouse wheel) moves the typed value by `Increment`, keeps the decimals of the value and the step (0.1 + 0.2 gives 0.3), clamps to `Minimum` and `Maximum`, and does nothing when it cannot move the value that way. `StepBy` is a `Func<decimal, StepAction, decimal>` that gives the step for the value and the direction. With `StepsValue="False"`, a step only raises `Step` with its `Action` (`Increment` or `Decrement`), and the app sets the value; `Step` is also raised for a step whose value the input's rules reject.
